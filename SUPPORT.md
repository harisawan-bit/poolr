## Methodology References

Poolr's calculations and workflow follow established international standards:
- **PRISMA 2020 Statement**: Page MJ, et al. *The PRISMA 2020 statement: an updated guideline for reporting systematic reviews.* BMJ 2021; 372:n71.
- **Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions (v6.4)**: Higgins JPT, et al. (eds). Cochrane, 2023.
- **GRADE Handbook**: Schünemann H, et al. *GRADE handbook for grading quality of evidence and strength of recommendations.* The GRADE Working Group, 2013.
- **robvis Tool**: McGuinness LA, Higgins JPT. *Risk-of-bias VISualization (robvis): An R package and Shiny web app for visualizing risk-of-bias assessments.* Research Synthesis Methods 2021; 12(1):55-61.
- **R metafor**: Viechtbauer W. *Conducting meta-analyses in R with the metafor package.* Journal of Statistical Software 2010; 36(3):1-48.
